# Alice in Borderland 🎮

**Alice in Borderland** is one of my favorite series. It’s a Japanese show about a group of people trapped in an empty Tokyo where they must play dangerous games to survive. The story is full of action, mystery, and smart thinking that keeps me hooked every episode.

---

## 🎭 Main Characters
1. Arisu (Ryohei Arisu)  
2. Usagi (Yuzuha Usagi)  
3. Chishiya  
4. Kuina  
5. Niragi  

---

### 💥 Why I Like It
- The games are creative and intense.  
- The characters show teamwork and survival skills.  
- It makes you think about life and friendship.  

> “In this world, you have to fight to survive.” – Arisu
